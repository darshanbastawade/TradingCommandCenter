# M38.8 — Native C# / vectorbt strategy parity closure

M38.8 makes the vectorbt research worker screen the same five signal hypotheses as authoritative native C#. The Python port implements the native EMA seed, Wilder ATR/ADX/+DI/-DI initialization, previous rolling-volume semantics, session VWAP, exchange-local sessions and inclusive-start/exclusive-end entry windows.

The exact native conditions are ported for `vwap-ema-trend-breakout-v1`, `opening-range-breakout-v1`, `ema-pullback-continuation-v1`, `vwap-reclaim-rejection-v1` and `adx-trend-continuation-v1`. Opening-range breakout has no invented EMA filter. Each other strategy includes its native trend, VWAP, ADX, DI, pullback/crossing, breakout and volume gates.

`VectorbtStrategyParityTests` builds deterministic C# fixtures with indicator warmup, exchange-local session changes, gaps, long and short setups, and an entry-window boundary near miss. It exports the exact candles, parameters, expected indicators and expected signals, invokes `workers/vectorbt/parity.py`, and requires 100% agreement on signal UTC timestamp and direction. Indicator values are compared to native Decimal results.

The checked `workers/vectorbt/parity-manifest.json` binds every passing strategy to `native-signals-v1` and the normalized SHA-256 of `parity.py`. Both the parity harness and worker verify the manifest. Changing the port therefore invalidates stale parity status. The worker emits schema-v2 parity evidence; M27 rejects missing, failed or strategy-mismatched evidence before persisting a sweep.

vectorbt remains research exploration only. The parity guarantee covers indicators and signal hypotheses, while C# remains authoritative for fills, costs, position sizing, backtests, qualification, risk and execution. M28 still reruns selected candidates through the native engine.
