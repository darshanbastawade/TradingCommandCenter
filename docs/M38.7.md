# M38.7 — Executable-price paper trading

M38.7 aligns M22 long-option paper execution with executable quote semantics and M17 option backtesting. Entry uses `BestAsk`; both stop/target detection and exit use `BestBid`. Adverse slippage is applied only after choosing that executable reference. When bid or ask exists, LTP cannot override it.

The default `requireBestBidAsk` policy fails closed when entry ask or an evaluated exit bid is unavailable. The explicit `allowLastPriceFallback` policy supports lower-quality research captures. New schema-v2 paper results record the selected policy, and completed trades record their actual entry and exit price sources.

This prevents a high LTP from creating a target fill while the sellable bid remains below the target, and prevents a low LTP from creating a stop fill while bid remains above the stop. Bid/ask spread and adverse slippage now flow directly into realized paper P&L.

Legacy M22 artifacts omit the new nullable evidence fields during serialization, preserving their existing SHA-256 representation. New evidence is included in the enclosing immutable artifact hash. No database migration is required.
