# M15 — Strategy scoring and ranking

M15 turns supplied OOS evidence into a transparent 0–100 score and applies hard qualification gates. The weighted score is:

| Component | Points |
|---|---:|
| Average net R | 20 |
| Net profit factor | 15 |
| Closed-equity drawdown | 15 |
| Walk-forward profitable-fold rate | 20 |
| Parameter stability | 15 |
| Profitable-regime rate | 10 |
| Trade sample size | 5 |

Values are clamped to each component's documented scale, so one extreme metric cannot dominate the result. Default qualification requires at least 30 trades, average net R of 0.10, profit factor of 1.20, drawdown no greater than 20%, profitable walk-forward folds of at least 60%, parameter stability of at least 70/100, and profitable regimes of at least 50%.

Average net R receives full component credit at 0.50R, profit factor at 2.0, and sample size at 60 trades. Walk-forward and regime rates use their full 0–100% ranges, robustness uses 0–100, and drawdown credit decreases linearly from zero drawdown to the configured maximum.

Every failed gate appears as a stable reason code. Ranking is deterministic by score and strategy ID. `SelectedStrategies` contains only qualified entries and defaults to at most two; it can contain zero or one. A high score cannot override a failed gate.

`StrategyRankingResult` is schema-versioned and JSON-serializable. The ranker consumes evidence; it does not silently run backtests, tune strategies, persist reports, or authorize orders. Live ranking should use comparable OOS windows, cost models and risk settings for all strategies.

M15 adds no database table, migration, AI call, broker connection or execution authority.
