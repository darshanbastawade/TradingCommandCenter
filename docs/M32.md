# M32 — Strategy Certificate V2

M32 issues one portable certificate for one selected strategy and binds it to the complete deterministic evidence chain: M16 research, an M24 sealed specification, the authoritative M25 native run, the M30 native/LEAN comparison, and the M31 robustness suite. Every input is hash-verified and must describe the same specification and dataset.

```powershell
dotnet run --project src/Trading.Api -- issue-certificate-v2 `
  --research .\artifacts\research-integrity.json `
  --specification .\artifacts\backtest-specification.sealed.json `
  --native .\artifacts\backtest-run.native.json `
  --comparison .\artifacts\cross-engine-comparison.json `
  --robustness .\artifacts\robustness-suite.json `
  --output .\artifacts\strategy-certificate-v2.json
```

The specification strategy must appear in the research artifact's selected ranking and have M16 strategy evidence. M30 must reference the supplied native result, and M31 must reference the same native result, specification, declared dataset and consumed market-data hashes. The parameter dictionary receives its own canonical SHA-256 in addition to the sealed specification hash.

The committed default policy requires:

- a qualified M15 selected ranking with no failure codes;
- an M30 `pass` verdict, 100% matched trades, exact entry/exit timestamps, at most 1 bp price deviation, at most 1% net-P&L deviation and at most 0.5 percentage points closed-trade drawdown deviation;
- Monte Carlo 95th-percentile closed-trade drawdown no greater than 20%;
- bootstrap loss probability no greater than 20%, ruin probability no greater than 5%, and 5th-percentile net P&L at least zero;
- positive net P&L at 10 additional slippage bps per side, 1.5× recorded costs, and a modeled 30-second entry delay;
- positive median net P&L with at least 10% randomly missed trades.

Thresholds are configured under `StrategyCertificateV2`. A missing required scenario is invalid evidence and stops issuance. A complete but failing evidence set still produces an immutable `evidenceRejected` certificate with stable failure codes and returns exit code `4`. This retains negative research evidence instead of silently discarding it.

An `evidenceQualified` V2 certificate is eligible only for M34 review. It is explicitly ineligible for paper trading, semi-live trading and direct-live trading. The certificate ID is deterministically derived from its policy and evidence identities, and the complete payload has its own SHA-256. M32 adds no database migration or broker operation; preserve the emitted JSON with the other immutable research artifacts.

M38.9 additionally requires genuine LEAN provenance carried by the M30 artifact. A comparison made from fixture, manually authored or legacy parity-only `lean` evidence receives `genuine-lean-validation-missing` and cannot become `evidenceQualified`.
