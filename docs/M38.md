# M38 — Production integrity closure

M38 closes and tests the software enforcement chain from certified research evidence to the single guarded direct-live broker call. It does not establish that a strategy is profitable, suitable for deployment, or guaranteed to remain profitable.

## Production invariant

> There exists no direct-live order path to `ILiveBrokerClient.PlaceLimitBuyAsync` that bypasses verified, fresh, exact-action M37 authorization, atomic one-time authorization consumption, and a fresh M18 deterministic risk approval.

M38.11 enforces this invariant in two ways:

1. an architecture assertion scans the production source and requires exactly one direct `PlaceLimitBuyAsync` call site, in `LiveTradingCommands.RunAsync`, after M37 verification, M18 preparation, and authorization consumption;
2. an end-to-end integration fixture reaches that call through the complete evidence chain and proves every listed single-gate failure leaves the fake broker's submission count at zero.

## Accepted evidence chain

The positive fixture models and verifies:

```text
M16 certified dataset/research
  -> M27 retained vectorbt candidate
  -> M28 authoritative native verification
  -> M30 sealed genuine-LEAN-provenance comparison
  -> M31 sealed robustness evidence
  -> M32 evidenceQualified certificate
  -> M33 certificate-bound analysis fixture
  -> M34 explicit human review
  -> post-M34 schema-v2 M22 paper sessions
  -> M35 durable qualification
  -> M36 authoritative reconciliation provenance
  -> M37 DirectSubmissionEligible authorization
  -> fresh M18 calendar-bound risk approval
  -> M23 one-time reservation and fake-broker submission
```

The LEAN and analyst payloads are previously sealed deterministic fixture evidence. The suite does not invoke LEAN, Docker, Azure OpenAI, Zerodha, SQL Server, or any network endpoint.

## Rejection matrix

The acceptance theory breaks exactly one condition per case and requires zero calls to `PlaceLimitBuyAsync` for:

- dataset or specification identity mismatch;
- non-passing M30 comparison;
- failed M31 robustness qualification;
- rejected or expired M32 evidence;
- invalid M34 human qualification;
- rejected M35 paper qualification;
- divergent M36 reconciliation;
- non-direct, expired, replayed, or unresolved-state M37 authorization;
- rejected M18 sizing/risk;
- the M23 kill switch, direct-order opt-in, or operator confirmation;
- stale quote or account evidence; and
- an unexpected existing broker position.

The suite also found and closed an M32 CLI serialization defect: its response previously exposed two members that both mapped to `status` under camel-case JSON. The command now emits distinct `eventStatus` and `certificateStatus` fields.

## Meaning of completion

M38 completion means the implemented authorization and evidence controls are closed and continuously tested with deterministic fakes. It does not replace operational reconciliation, exchange/broker monitoring, credential controls, deployment review, or human responsibility. It does not imply profitable trading.
